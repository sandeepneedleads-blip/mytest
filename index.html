Personal Information

Amandeep Singh, the visionary founder of 5 Trainers, embarked on a mission to revolutionize education. Hailing from Punjab, Amandeep drew inspiration from the state's name, which symbolizes one of the five essential elements that shape our lives – 'Punj' meaning Five and 'Aab' meaning Water.

The name '5 Trainers' is rooted in the concept of five essentials, mirroring the harmony found in nature's five elements and the five human senses. At 5 Trainers, we stand strong on the foundation of the 5 T's:

Training, Technology, Thinking, Time and Teamwork

Just as education is crucial for growth, personal development is equally important. Our founder, Mr. Amandeep, embodies this philosophy, nurturing students to become well-rounded individuals equipped for success.

The institute offers digital marketing and IT core courses complemented by Personality Development, Motivation, Creative Thinking, and Networking Skills. With a passion for empowering students, Amandeep Singh's mission is to shape the next generation of leaders who can make a meaningful difference in society. Through 5 Trainers, he is redefining the profile of education and impacting a better future.

Under the leadership of Mr. Amandeep Singh, 5 Trainers' commitment to holistic education never disappoints. His qualities in building an institute not only enhance a student's educational growth but also their personal growth. The aim of producing professionals comes with better human attributes. He focuses on out-of-the-box thinking, which creates positive outcomes.
